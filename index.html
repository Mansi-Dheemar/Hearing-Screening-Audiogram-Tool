Welcome

This tool performs a pure-tone hearing screening test across 6 standard audiometric frequencies (250 Hz – 8000 Hz) for both ears, and generates an AI-powered report using the WHO hearing-loss grading scale.

  • 🎧 Use a pair of stereo headphones (earbuds or over-ear)
  • 🤫 Find a quiet environment
  • 🔉 Set device volume to a comfortable level
  • ⏱️ The test takes approximately 3–5 minutes
🔊
